About Me

My name is Chuanneng Sun. I am an Applied Scientist II at Amazon Stores Foundation AI, where I work on Rufus foundation models, agentic LLM post-training, reinforcement learning, synthetic data generation, and large-scale agent rollouts.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Rutgers University, advised by Prof. Dario Pompili. My research focuses on multi-agent reinforcement learning, federated learning, robotics, edge intelligence, and LLM-based decision-making. Before Rutgers, I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Hefei University of Technology.

For more details, please see my Google Scholar or LinkedIn.
